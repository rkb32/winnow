Train/Test Leaks

Test photos that also appear in your training photos. The model has already seen them, so its test score will look better than it really is.

Fix: delete the training copy of each pair (the download above does this), then re-measure your test score.

Duplicate Photos

Two files that are really the same picture, keeping both can trick a model into looking more accurate than it really is.

Fix: keep one photo from each pair. The download above drops the extras Claude marked Remove.

Blurry Photos

Too out of focus to be useful for training.

Fix: drop them, or retake them if that class is short on examples.

Rotation Problems

Photos with a hidden "rotate me" tag. If your labels were drawn on the unrotated photo, they may no longer line up. Winnow can't see your label files, so these are worth checking by hand.

Fix: open a few in your labeling tool. If the boxes are off, re-save the photos with rotation applied (for example Pillow's ImageOps.exif_transpose) and redraw those boxes.

Couldn't Be Scanned

Files that failed to open, likely corrupted or not actually images.
