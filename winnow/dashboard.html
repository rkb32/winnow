What Winnow checks for

Upload photos above, or load the example set, then press Scan to see your results here.

Train/Test Leaks

Test photos that also appear in your training photos. The model has already seen them, so its test score will look better than it really is.

Fix: delete the training copy of each pair (the download above does this), then re-measure your test score.

Train/Test Leaks — none found.

Train/Test Shift

Your test photos look like a different kind of picture than your training photos, so a good test score may not carry over to real use.

Fix: add test photos that look like what the model will really see, or add training photos like the odd ones.

Train/Test Shift — none found.

Duplicate Photos

Two files that are really the same picture, keeping both can trick a model into looking more accurate than it really is.

Fix: keep one photo from each pair. The download above drops the extras Claude marked Remove.

Duplicate Photos — none found.

Same-Moment Shots

Photos taken seconds apart on one camera. Their pixels can differ while the moment is the same, so spreading them across train and test inflates the score.

Fix: keep one photo per moment, and never split a moment between train and test.

Same-Moment Shots — none found.

Blurry Photos

Too out of focus to be useful for training.

Fix: drop them, or retake them if that class is short on examples.

Blurry Photos — none found.

Rotation Problems

Photos with a hidden "rotate me" tag. If your labels were drawn on the unrotated photo, they may no longer line up. Add your label files above and Winnow checks this for you; otherwise these are worth checking by hand.

Fix: open a few in your labeling tool. If the boxes are off, re-save the photos with rotation applied (for example Pillow's ImageOps.exif_transpose) and redraw those boxes.

Rotation Problems — none found.

Watermarks & Stock Photos

The same logo, watermark or frame in many photos (a model can learn it instead of the subject), or a stock agency named in a photo's own metadata (its license may not allow training).

Fix: crop the overlay out or replace those photos, and check the license of any stock photo before you train on it.

Watermarks & Stock Photos — none found.

AI-Generated Photos

Photos whose own metadata says an AI tool made them. Winnow only reads what a file declares, so a photo with no marker isn't proof it's real.

Fix: decide whether generated images belong in your training set, and keep them out of your test set.

AI-Generated Photos — none found.

Label Problems

Boxes that leave the photo, have no area or repeat, label sizes that don't match the photo, and photos with no label.

Fix: open the flagged photos in your labeling tool and fix those boxes.

Label Problems — none found.

Couldn't Be Scanned

Files that failed to open, likely corrupted or not actually images.

Fix: re-export or re-download these files, a model can't learn from them.

Couldn't Be Scanned — none found.
